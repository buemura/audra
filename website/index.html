What you read stays on your Mac.

Nothing is stored or sent
Audra never saves the text it reads and never sends it anywhere. Speech is generated on your Mac.
One download, once
The only time Audra goes online is to download the natural voice model (about 190 MB) the first time it opens.
Your clipboard comes back
Some apps, like Chrome and Electron apps, don't share what's selected. For those, Audra copies the selection and then puts your previous clipboard back right away.

Install Audra

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later on a Mac with Apple Silicon.

Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/buemura/audra/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Installs the latest release into Applications and opens it. It checks the download's checksum, and macOS won't block the first launch. Run it again to update.

Then grant Accessibility access

Audra asks for it when it first opens. It needs this to read what you've selected in other apps. You'll find it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

Questions

Reading stopped working after an update

macOS sometimes drops the Accessibility permission when the app changes. Reset it in Terminal, then open Audra and grant access again:

tccutil reset Accessibility com.buemura.Audra
Does it work in Chrome, Slack or VS Code?

Yes. These apps don't share their selection with macOS, so Audra presses ⌘C, reads the copied text and then restores your clipboard. Clipboard-history tools may still record that copy.

How do I change the shortcut or the voice?

Click the Audra icon in the menu bar and choose Settings…. You can set the shortcut, the speed and a voice for each language there.

How much disk space do the voices use?

About 190 MB. Download progress shows in the menu bar menu and in Settings → General. To free the space, click Remove next to Natural voices in Settings → General. Audra then uses your Mac's voices.

Can I get better voices for other languages?

Yes. Download Enhanced or Premium voices in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices. Audra uses them for languages its natural voices don't cover.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

Not yet. Windows and Linux versions are on the roadmap, along with a notarized Mac build.
